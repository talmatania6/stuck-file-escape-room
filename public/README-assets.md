# Assets

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `companion/sheet.jpg` | The companion's 6-angle character sheet (FRONT, LEFT 3/4, LEFT PROFILE, BACK, RIGHT PROFILE, RIGHT 3/4 – left to right, equal widths). Already in place. The game cuts it into the six angles and picks one by where the camera stands; the speech-bubble portrait is cropped from FRONT. `.png`/`.webp` also work. |
| `audio/music-calm.mp3` | Music, more than 10 minutes left (e.g. made in Suno). |
| `audio/music-mid.mp3` | Music, 5–10 minutes left. |
| `audio/music-high.mp3` | Music, under 5 minutes left. |

All three music files must exist for them to be used; until then a quiet synthesized drone plays.
All in-game text is in `src/game/rooms.js`.
